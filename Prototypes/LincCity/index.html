Welcome to LINC

Build. Connect. Endure.

Time starts paused. There is no rush.

  • Select a tool, then tap the map. Streets can be painted by dragging. Buildings must touch a street that leads to the central core.
  • Add two Habitats, an Exchange, a Power core, Waterworks, a Clinic, and a Rift ward. The starting streets leave space beside them.
  • Press Advance cycle to collect revenue, welcome residents, and update city stability. The play button advances every 8 seconds.
  • Hold 32 residents, full services, positive income, stability of at least 70%, and pressure below 45% for four cycles to win.
  • A Void surge arrives every fifth cycle. Wards reduce pressure. High taxes reduce stability; extra clinic and ward funding improves their output and costs more.
  • Use Save for a local checkpoint. On a keyboard: 1–8 select tools, arrows select tiles, Enter builds, and Space advances when the map is not focused.
